LEGAL & TRANSPARENCY

Privacy policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026. Review and adapt this policy to match the services enabled on the live site.

Important: this is a starter policy template, not legal advice. Update it before enabling analytics, advertising, third-party comments, accounts or other data collection.

1. Information this site handles

The initial WasmStore front end does not require account registration. Game comment and touch-control preferences may be stored in local browser storage on the device where the user sets them. These preferences are not a shared account or server-side record.

2. Contact messages

The starter contact form prepares an email draft in the visitor's email application once a real address is configured. The current template does not send submissions to or store them on a WasmStore server. If a hosted form service is added, identify the provider and describe its data handling here.

3. Cookies, analytics and advertising

Do not claim that analytics, advertising or tracking cookies are absent after adding third-party scripts. Before enabling those services, list the providers, purposes, data categories, retention periods and available opt-out or consent controls. Follow the laws applicable to your audience.

4. Third-party game providers and links

Game embeds, external links, comment widgets, video players and other third-party integrations may collect information under their own policies. Review their terms and privacy notices before integration.

5. Data retention and security

Browser-only preferences remain until cleared by the user or browser. Any future server-side data collection must have a defined retention period, access controls and a process for handling deletion requests.

6. Children's privacy

If the site is directed to children or knowingly collects personal information from children, add the age-appropriate notices and safeguards required by applicable law before launch.

7. Your choices and contact

Users can clear site data through their browser settings. For privacy questions, use the Contact page after the site's contact details have been configured.

8. Policy updates

We may update this notice when the site changes. Publish the correct effective date whenever material changes are made.
