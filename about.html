OUR STORY

About WasmStore

A thoughtfully organized home for browser gaming, game discovery and useful gaming knowledge.

What is WasmStore?

WasmStore is an independent browser gaming directory designed to help players discover web-playable games, explore genres and learn about the technology that brings games to browsers. The library may include classic games, action titles, racing games, puzzles and experimental web projects.

Our approach

We aim to clearly label game availability, provide useful context before a player launches a title, and publish practical guides. A listing is not a guarantee that a game is currently playable: each game needs a working, tested source and appropriate distribution rights before its launch can be enabled.

Technology and compatibility

Browser games may use HTML5, JavaScript, WebGL, WebAssembly or other technologies. Requirements vary by title and device. We do not claim that every game works on every browser, mobile device or Chromebook.

Independence and trademarks

WasmStore is an independent directory and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Rockstar Games, id Software or any other game publisher unless explicitly stated. Game names and trademarks remain the property of their respective owners.
